# PRODUCT.md

## Product

Moonlaugh is a small software studio in Hanoi, Vietnam, founded by Phan Đức Thịnh (Bryan).
It builds iOS and Android apps, websites and web apps, AI and machine-learning features,
data science and analytics, Android TV / video streaming apps, and does business analysis.

This repository is the studio's **public website**. It is a sales surface (mode: *Persuade*).

## Audience

1. **Foreign clients** (small companies, startups, agencies; Europe, Middle East, Southeast Asia, Latin America),
   arriving from LinkedIn, Upwork or referrals. They read in English, check whether the studio is real
   and trustworthy, then contact.
2. **Vietnamese clients** (startups, SMEs, outsourcing companies), arriving from Facebook, TikTok and referrals.
   They read in Vietnamese and prefer Zalo or Messenger.
3. **Developers and students** from the Moonlaugh TikTok, fanpage and Facebook group.

## What success looks like

- A visitor understands within one screen what Moonlaugh does and how to start.
- A visitor can contact the studio in one step, through the channel they already use.
- Nothing on the site overclaims: no invented clients, numbers or testimonials.

## Voice

Plain, specific, calm. Short sentences. Facts over adjectives ("tested on real devices",
not "world-class"). Same meaning in English and Vietnamese, written naturally in each.

## Constraints

- Free or near-free to run (static hosting, Web3Forms free plan).
- Maintained part-time by one engineer with AI coding agents: documentation and tests are part of the product.
- The founder works full-time elsewhere: never show employer names, code or confidential details.
